# Open positions

#### PhD Applications

Prospective PhD students should apply through the [DKFZ International PhD Program](https://www.dkfz.de/en/phd-program/https://www.dkfz.de/en/career/international-phd-program) or the [EMBL International PhD Program](https://www.embl.org/about/info/embl-international-phd-programme). Currently, we are also recruiting through the [Max Planck School of Biomedical AI](https://biomedicalai.maxplanckschools.org/4056/application-process).
We are unable to consider unsolicited PhD applications sent directly to the lab.


#### Postdoctoral Applications

Prospective postdoctoral researchers are encouraged to apply through the [DKFZ Postdoctoral Fellowship](https://www.dkfz.de/karriere/international-postdoc-program) or [EMBL Postdoctoral Programme](https://www.embl.org/about/info/postdoctoral-programme/).
If you do not apply through one of the specific programs above, please send a CV, a letter of motivation and ideally 2 references in a single pdf directly to [office [at] steglelab.org](mailto:office@steglelab.org). Letter of interest should be tailored to our group, mentioning projects of our group that you find interesting, and explaining how you would fit in our group. Additional supporting documents demonstrating your prior experience incl. code samples of previous projects and links to a GitHub repository are very welcome.


#### BSc/MSc thesis and internship applications

Students interested in a BSc or MSc thesis project, or a research internship, are welcome to contact us at [office [at] steglelab.org](mailto:office@steglelab.org). Please send a CV and a motivation letter tailored to the research interests of the lab, and include information about your envisioned starting date and duration.<br>
Due to the high number of applications, we review these applications every two months. The cutoff dates are: 31 January, 31 March, 31 May, 31 July, 30 September and 30 November. Candidates will be notified within a few weeks of each cutoff date.


#### General information

If you are applying for any position in the lab, including internships and thesis projects, please include the signed [data consent form (PDF)](https://steglelab.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Consent-to-Process-Your-Application-Data.pdf) with your application. We can only process your application once we have received it.
